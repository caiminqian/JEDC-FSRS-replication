Dear Editor,

We submit our manuscript for consideration as a research article in the *Journal of Economic Dynamics and Control*. This paper introduces a novel cognitive memory engine based on SuperMemo's SM-20 and SM-2 algorithms into agent-based financial modeling, bridging cognitive psychology and computational economics.

**Key contributions**:
1. We implement SM-20 (forgetting curve) and SM-2 (quality-based interval scheduling) within economic agents, creating realistic memory decay and consolidation dynamics.
2. We model memory contagion via network information flow, demonstrating systemic amplification of risk perceptions.
3. We calibrate model parameters (memory half-life S=45 days, contagion strength α=0.6) to five historical market crashes (1987, 2000, 2008, 2020, 2022).
4. We formulate the regulator's intervention problem as a POMDP and solve it via systematic grid search after RL methods fail. The optimal "early mild intervention" policy (threshold 0.55, severity 0.20) yields a 1740% improvement over baseline on 30 agents and 374% improvement on 1000 agents.
5. We validate robustness across network densities and memory decay rates, showing the optimal policy generalizes widely.
